--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(A) CLUSTERED PERMUTATION TESTS, WITH A FAMILY-WISE CORRECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Signs are flipped per SERIES, never per test point, so dependent rolling
origins are not treated as independent draws. Holm-Bonferroni across the
arms in the family. The paired arms are one-sided by construction where
arm B contains arm A, so a two-sided p is conservative here and that is the
direction to err in.

The arm(s) that do NOT survive, named rather than dropped quietly:
    statsforecast m=50 (m1)          delta +0.0120  raw p 0.08155  Holm p 0.08155
These are the smallest effects in the family, which is what a
family-wise correction is for. The disclosure can now say what
survives correction and what does not, instead of declining to
correct and asserting that it would not have mattered.

Two limits, stated. The committed outputs carry cell means and standard
errors rather than per-series differences, so the permutation runs on
values matched to that mean and s.e.; the resulting p is an upper bound on
what the raw differences would give. And the exactly-zero cells are omitted
from the family because a test of a difference that is identically zero by
construction has no null to reject.

(C) THE SAME ONE-RANK MISS, ABSOLUTELY AND AS AN EXCEEDANCE RATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A helper one rank short delivers (k*-1)/(n+1) rather than k*/(n+1). The
absolute coverage miss is 1/(n+1) at every level. The exceedance rate a
risk or alerting report states is 1 - coverage, and THERE the same miss is
a multiplicative factor that grows as the level tightens.
