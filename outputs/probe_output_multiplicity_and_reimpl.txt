--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(A) CLUSTERED PERMUTATION TESTS, WITH A FAMILY-WISE CORRECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Signs are sign-changed by SERIES cluster, not by test point, so rolling
origins do not masquerade as separate units. Holm-Bonferroni across the
arms in the family. The paired arms are one-sided by construction where
arm B contains arm A, so a two-sided p is conservative here and that is the
direction to err in.

THE DIFFERENCES ARE THE ARMS' OWN, NOT A RECONSTRUCTION. An earlier version
of this section sign-flipped synthetic draws calibrated to each cell's reported mean
and standard error, because the per-series differences looked absent from the
committed outputs. They are not absent. Each arm contributes one
point per series; its per-series entry falls in {-1, 0, +1}, and the
printed gains and losses determine that vector. Every cell below is
parsed out of the file named beside it, and the parse asserts that the
printed delta equals (gains - losses)/units -- a rolling-origin arm
fails there instead of being permuted as though it had one point per series.

The p is then exact rather than simulated. Sign flipping moves only the
nonzero entries, so P(|S| >= |gains - losses|) for S a sum of Rademachers is
a binomial tail with nothing left to estimate. The simulation is kept beside
it as a check and not as the answer, because it cannot resolve below
1/20001 = 5.000e-05 and the smallest of these is well under that.

7 of 8 arms remain significant at 0.05 after Holm-Bonferroni across the family.

The arm(s) that do NOT survive, named rather than dropped quietly:
    statsforecast m=50 (m1)          delta +0.0120  raw p 0.25000  Holm p 0.25000
These are the smallest effects in the family, which is what a
family-wise correction is for. The disclosure can now say what
survives correction and what does not, instead of declining to
correct and asserting that it would not have mattered.

One limit, stated. The exactly-zero cells are omitted from the family,
because a difference that is identically zero by construction has no null
to reject and carrying it would inflate the family to weaken the correction
applied to the rest. The coincidence cells of the audit's severity section
are that case, and they are evidence by being zero rather than by a p.

(C) THE SAME ONE-RANK MISS, ABSOLUTELY AND AS AN EXCEEDANCE RATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A helper one rank short delivers (k*-1)/(n+1) rather than k*/(n+1). The
one-rank coverage change is 1/(n+1) at every level. Risk and alerting
reports usually print 1 - coverage; there, that same miss becomes
a larger rate multiplier at tighter requested levels.
