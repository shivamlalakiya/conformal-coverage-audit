W12  THE TAIL LAW FOR pi -- what the fractional-rank result left open
==========================================================================================================

  pi = E[F(T)-F(a)] / E[F(b)-F(a)]   (a RATIO of expectations)
  i  = n - floor(h)  is the DEPTH of the landed-in gap from the top
  (G) exponential tail:  pi = (i+1) gamma / (i + gamma)      -- no
      tail parameter, exact
  (F) Pareto(theta):     pi = (i+1) E[1-(1+gamma(R-1))^-theta],
      R ~ Pareto(i theta)
  both -> gamma as i -> inf, at rate O(1/i): the interior result, with
  the right variable identified. Depth, not n.
  reps 300000   seed 20260805   Pareto theta 1.5

    The practical rule: a requested level puts you in the tail regime when
    (1-alpha) is close enough to 1 that fewer than a handful of order
    statistics sit above the virtual index. That is a statement about
    n(1-alpha), not about n, which is why collecting more data does not
    leave the regime if the level rises with it.

==========================================================================================================
SUMMARY
==========================================================================================================
  pi has a closed form in both extreme-value domains, exact to
  0.00057 against measurement. The Gumbel form (i+1)gamma/(i+gamma)
  carries no tail parameter, so it is usable without estimating anything.
  Both reduce to pi = gamma at rate O(1/i), which identifies DEPTH rather
  than sample size as the variable governing the interior approximation.
  Replacing gamma by pi cuts the mean coverage prediction error from
  0.00124 to 0.00026, and on the
  shallow gaps where the manuscript's caveat lived, by more.
