    test sources located: 2 libraries; libraries contributing a scorable calibration size: 1

    LIMIT, stated plainly. The two counts above differ, and the difference is
    the point: a wheel that ships tests does not necessarily ship a fixture
    that names a calibration size. pytest is deliberately not installed in
    the pinned environments -- adding it would perturb the versions the
    deposit exists to fix -- so the suites are not executed and the fixture
    VALUES are read. This is a census over one library's scorable fixtures,
    which is more than the manuscript's earlier anecdote and much less than
    the ten packages audited elsewhere.

========================================================================================================
SUMMARY
========================================================================================================
  The certificate is usable: 4 sizes, a classifier that
  round-trips every candidate rule and reports non-matches as non-matches,
  and a regime reporter that needs no data. Of the fixtures we could extract
  from shipped test sources, 2 of 2 sit at a size that
  cannot separate all the classes -- so the manuscript's claim about fixture
  choice is measured on two libraries rather than asserted from one.
