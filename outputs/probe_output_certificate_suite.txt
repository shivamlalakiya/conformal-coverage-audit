    LIMIT, stated plainly. Only two of the ten audited packages ship their
    tests in the wheel, so this census covers two libraries and a handful of
    fixtures, not the ten. pytest is deliberately not installed in the
    pinned environments -- adding it would perturb the versions the deposit
    exists to fix -- so the suites are not executed and the fixture VALUES
    are read. A census over two libraries is still the difference between
    the manuscript's anecdote and a measurement, and it is not more than
    that.

========================================================================================================
SUMMARY
========================================================================================================
  The certificate is usable: 4 sizes, a classifier that
  round-trips every candidate rule and reports non-matches as non-matches,
  and a regime reporter that needs no data. Of the fixtures we could extract
  from shipped test sources, 3 of 3 sit at a size that
  cannot separate all the classes -- so the manuscript's claim about fixture
  choice is measured on two libraries rather than asserted from one.
