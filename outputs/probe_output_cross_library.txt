--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(ii) A CONVENTION THE TAXONOMY DOES NOT CONTAIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    scipy.stats.mstats.mquantiles defaults to alphap = betap = 0.4, the
    Cunnane plotting position. That pair is not among Hyndman and Fan's
    nine definitions, so it is not a row in the rank map.
      virtual index at the raw level : 45.5800
      delivered coverage h/(n+1)     : 0.8937   against a requested 0.90
      distribution-free guarantee    : 0.8824
      corrected level it needs       : 0.9064

    Its delivered coverage differs from every numpy method (asserted in
    self_check), so a caller who reaches for scipy rather than numpy gets a
    fourteenth convention with its own corrected level. The rank map is a
    map of the nine definitions; the space of SHIPPED conventions is larger.
    The fractional-rank result covers it anyway, because it is stated in
    terms of the virtual index rather than of a named definition -- which is
    the argument for stating it that way.

==============================================================================================================
SUMMARY
==============================================================================================================
  15 shipped API configurations across three packages, 7 distinct
  virtual indices, and one default convention -- scipy's -- that is not in
  the Hyndman-Fan taxonomy at all. numpy and pandas agree with each other by
  construction; scipy does not agree with either at its own default. The
  interface claim is therefore stronger than the manuscript states, and the
  right way to state it is in terms of the virtual index.
  R, Julia, Octave/MATLAB and pyspark are NOT measured here and no claim is
  made about them beyond the arithmetic transfer recorded in block (iii).
