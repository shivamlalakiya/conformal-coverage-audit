W14  ONE LAW FOR pi -- the two cases of W12 are a single family
========================================================================================================

  pi(xi, i, gamma) = (i+1) E_{s~Beta(i,1)}[1 - (1+gamma(s^-xi -1))^(-1/xi)]
  xi = extreme-value shape index; i = gap depth; gamma = interpolation
  fraction. p = 1-F(V_(j)) cancels, so pi depends on the distribution
  ONLY through xi.
  reps 300000   seed 20260805

    xi = -1 point of this law rather than a lucky choice.

========================================================================================================
SUMMARY
========================================================================================================
  pi depends on the distribution only through the extreme-value shape xi.
  One formula covers all three domains; W12's two forms are its xi = 0 and
  xi > 0 sections, and the Weibull domain xi < 0 was previously unreached.
  pi = gamma holds exactly at xi = -1 and nowhere else, so the flat-density
  condition of the earlier results IS a statement about the shape index.
  Measured to 0.00054 over 28 cells and seven distributions,
  against 0.16550 for the xi = 0 form.
