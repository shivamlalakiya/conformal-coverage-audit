SUMMARY
========================================================================================================
  the h/(n+1) prediction holds to |err| <= 0.00239 on 20 interior cells across 5 distributions,
  including Pareto(1.5). It degrades in the extreme upper tail, in the
  conservative direction, for the reason the decomposition predicts.
  Proposition 1 of the manuscript is the pi=0 corner of this statement and
  remains the correct DISTRIBUTION-FREE reading; it is not what the
  libraries deliver, and the difference is a whole rank at small n.
