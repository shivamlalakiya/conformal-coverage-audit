(3) THE BILL
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Neither substitution shrinks anything; both enlarge. That follows from how
the three positions are ordered and needs no measurement. The k* column
splits two regimes. When k* coincides with n the requirement names the
largest draw, one step up from the region where no index whatsoever meets
the request, and the quotient then describes the uppermost spacing of a
heavy-tailed sample instead of describing the definitions. Elsewhere the
charge is a few per cent and shrinks with n, as the intervening positions
crowd together.
