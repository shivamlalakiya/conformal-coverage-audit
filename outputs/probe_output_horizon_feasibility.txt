====================================================================================================
SUMMARY
====================================================================================================
  The feasibility floor is a property of the level RESOLVED, not the level
  REQUESTED. Any construction that divides alpha -- Bonferroni over a
  horizon, over output dimensions, over groups -- multiplies the floor.
  At H=12, alpha=0.05 the floor is 239 calibration windows; the audited
  default is 60 or fewer. The audit's two-window finding is the H=1 corner
  of this, not a curiosity.
