============================================================================================================
W15  GROUP-CONDITIONAL SEVERITY -- where the deficit stops being O(1/n)
============================================================================================================

  A total calibration budget of 2000 scores, split G ways. Every cell has
  the SAME total budget, so the only thing that changes is how finely it
  is divided. Groups are exponential-tailed (xi = 0 exactly) with scales
  rising across groups; scores are i.i.d. within a group, so per-group
  exchangeability holds and absolute coverage is attributable.
  4000 replications per cell   seed 20260805

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

============================================================================================================
SUMMARY
============================================================================================================
  Splitting a fixed calibration budget G ways shrinks the per-group size and
  the gap depth together, so the one-rank deficit and the discarded
  interpolation grow at once. Per-group coverage follows the shape law and a
  marginal-only account is systematically optimistic. The worst group, which
  is what a group-conditional guarantee is usually quoted as covering, is
  where the audit's O(1/n) objection stops applying.
