========================================================================================================
THE THREE-ITEM REPORTING CHECKLIST
========================================================================================================
Any paper or library that claims a distribution-free coverage level should
report these three numbers. They are short enough that a reviewer can demand
them and cheap enough that no author can object.

  1. The DELIVERED coverage, not the requested one: the rank the implementation lands on, divided by n+1.
  2. The RANK itself, as k of n, so a reader can check the arithmetic without rerunning anything.
  3. The CALIBRATION SET SIZE n, and whether any valid finite bound exists at that n for the requested level.

The point of the third item is that it is the only one that can say 'no valid
bound exists here'. Items 1 and 2 are always answerable; item 3 is the one
that catches a finite interval returned where none is possible.
