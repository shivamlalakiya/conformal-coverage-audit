  Read `worst n power` against the claim that pinning a test to one of
  these sizes tells you nothing: there the sixteen candidates yield only
  that many distinct observations between them, so a suite fixed at such a
  size misses the difference by construction rather than by luck.

  Scope: sixteen candidates, told apart. Where a helper matches none, the
  finding is a gap in this list -- a fact about the instrument and not a
  verdict on the library, which is the scope the classifier carries too.
