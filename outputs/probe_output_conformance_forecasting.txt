========================================================================================================
THE THREE-ITEM REPORTING CHECKLIST
========================================================================================================
Any paper or library that claims a distribution-free coverage level should
report these three numbers. They are short enough that a reviewer can demand
them and cheap enough that no author can object.

  1. The DELIVERED coverage, not the nominal coverage: the rank the implementation lands on, divided by n+1.
  2. The RANK itself, reported as k of n, letting a reader verify the arithmetic without re-executing anything.
  3. The CALIBRATION SET SIZE n, together with whether a valid finite bound can exist at that n for the nominated level.

The third item matters because it alone can declare 'no valid bound exists
here'. Items 1 and 2 are always answerable; item 3 is what flags a finite
interval handed back where no valid one can be constructed.
