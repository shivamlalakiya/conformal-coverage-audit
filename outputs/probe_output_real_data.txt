A positive delta means the required rank covers more than the shipped call.
`empirical` is two rails on the SIGNED residuals, so its arm B is the same
two-rail construction at the required SPAN and its landed figure is a span in
gaps. `conformal` is a symmetric band on the absolute ones and its arm B is a
rank. Arm B contains arm A in both, asserted per fit rather than argued.
Real series are not exchangeable, so an absolute miss is not attributable to
the convention on its own -- the paired delta is what carries the claim.
