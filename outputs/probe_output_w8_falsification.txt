====================================================================================================
VERDICT -- how wide the paper's claim may be
====================================================================================================
Read the three settings together, not separately:

  1. VaR         the mechanism reproduces outside conformal prediction, on
                 three differently-tailed distributions, and the measured
                 exceedance matches the exact 1 - k/(n+1) for the rank the
                 level lands on.
  2. Tolerance   the mechanism reproduces, and here it is WORSE than in
                 conformal prediction, because the required rank comes from a
                 binomial tail rather than from a level at all -- so no choice
                 of level recovers it.
  3. Bootstrap   the mechanism is present but NOT the dominant term. Read the
                 'index-map share' column before writing anything general.

So the frame that survives is the mechanism, not the severity:
  SAY      the level-to-rank map governs any distribution-free bound built
           from a sample quantile, and conformal prediction is where the
           consequence is sharpest because the guarantee is exact there.
  DO NOT   generalise the effect SIZES measured on conformal libraries to
           other settings. Setting 3 refutes that directly.
