========================================================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE, AND WHY NO RATE IS PRINTED
========================================================================================================
No prevalence appears anywhere above. The instrument that would have
produced one loses part of the census it was calibrated against, and the
loss is not a constant: it depends on how each package happens to move a
level from its public signature to its ordering call. A rate carrying
that error would be printed next to a count made by hand and would invite
precisely the comparison the error rules out.

The candidate counts are not an upper bound either. They over-generate,
because most lines matching an ordering call resolve nothing, and they
under-generate, for the four reasons anchored above.

What would produce a prevalence is the same work the census did: one
expression at a time, each ruled against the criterion and anchored, with
a second reader asked to break the ruling. This probe measures the size
of that job and does not do it.
