========================================================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
========================================================================================================
The blind stops an answer reaching the sheet. It does not stop a
determined reader looking one up: the labels sit in the census manifest in
this same repository and in the manuscript's own table, both public. What
the redaction buys is that nobody is told the answer by accident while
reading the item, which is the failure a worksheet actually has.

A sample of twelve bounds a kappa loosely, and one returned sheet more so.
The sample is also drawn from sites the audit already located, so it
measures agreement on classification and says nothing about whether the
census found every site there is. That second question is the frame
sweep's and the enumerator calibration's, not this one's.
