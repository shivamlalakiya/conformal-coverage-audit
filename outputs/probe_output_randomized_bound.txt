(iv) THE COST, stated rather than argued
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Under the floor some weight has to go on the vacuous bound. The

SUMMARY
====================================================================================================
  'No valid finite bound exists' must read 'no valid finite DETERMINISTIC
  bound exists'. Without a coin the reachable coverages are m+1 points
  1/(m+1) apart, so any level off that grid is out of reach at every m, not
  merely under the floor -- and mixing two neighbours lands on it exactly.
  One added word rescues the claim, and what to do instead belongs beside
  it.
