(iv) THE COST, stated rather than argued
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Below the feasibility floor the mixture must place weight on the
    vacuous bound, so the interval is infinite a known fraction of the time
    and, conditional on being finite, undercovers by construction:

SUMMARY
====================================================================================================
  'No valid finite bound exists' must read 'no valid finite DETERMINISTIC
  bound exists'. The deterministic attainable set is m+1 points spaced
  1/(m+1) apart, so a requested level off that grid is unattainable at every
  m and not only below the floor; randomising between neighbours attains it
  exactly. The manuscript's claim survives with one word added, and the
  constructive alternative belongs next to it.
