THE SHARE
====================================================================================================
audited monthly downloads           4,061,258
unaudited monthly downloads        16,116,290
combined                           20,177,548
audited share                           20.1%

over 10 audited and 12 unaudited packages the API answered for.

So the census covers a MINORITY of downloads in the set considered.
That is the honest figure and it is the one reported. The unaudited
side is dominated by general forecasting libraries whose conformal
path is one feature among many, which is why the share is low; a
denominator restricted to conformal-first packages would be higher
and would also be chosen to flatter.

THE NARROWER QUESTION, asked separately rather than instead. Restricted
to packages centred on conformal prediction:
  audited        245,601    unaudited       55,535    share  81.6%

Both figures are reported because each answers a different objection.
The broad one answers 'do these packages matter'; the narrow one
answers 'did you audit the conformal ecosystem or a sample of it'.

====================================================================================================
WHAT THIS DOES NOT SETTLE
====================================================================================================
A share of PyPI downloads says excludes r julia sql and spreadsheet workflows
users, and the interface census covers those separately without a
denominator of any kind. It says nothing about which code paths inside a
downloaded package are executed. And a package absent from PyPI entirely --
vendored, internal, or installed from source -- is invisible here.
